Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your financial data is personal. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have about your data.

01 Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you choose to record.
  • Basic usage information — such as login times, used to keep your account secure.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers or connect to your financial institutions.

02 How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Provide, maintain and improve Spendly's features.
  • Show you summaries and breakdowns of your own spending.
  • Authenticate you and protect your account from unauthorised access.
  • Contact you about important changes to the service or these policies.

We never sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

03 Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers and is accessible only to you through your account. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never visible to us.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your expense records and personal information are permanently removed.

04 Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted providers, such as hosting and font services, to operate the website. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

We do not share your expense data with third parties except where required by law.

05 Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to access, correct or delete your data, please reach out to us at privacy@example.com.
